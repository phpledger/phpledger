The owner decision of 21 September recorded in [DEVELOPMENT.md](../DEVELOPMENT.md#fixed-assets-13-m14) supersedes that wording: charge a whole month in the month of entry into service, regardless of arrival day, and no charge in the disposal month. There is intentionally no convention column. A second convention requires a separate decision and schema scope. This clarification does not satisfy the outstanding independent accounting-review acceptance or close #95.
