The exact-decimal representation, immutable storage, permissions, locking and duplicate-prevention mechanisms below are **PHP Ledger engineering controls** supporting reliable accounting. ## Review record and acceptance

**CORE-12 — Physical cash availability, local implementation.** `cash_bank` accounts have an explicit nullable `money_kind` introduced by migration 051. Existing accounts and a new combined starter account remain unclassified; their money-out postings require an audited physical-cash or bank decision. No classification is inferred from a name or code. The central ledger guard compares exact posted daily closing balances under the existing book lock, rejects newly negative or worsened physical-cash balances on the posting date or later affected dates, and allows funding that improves historical deficits. Same-day entries aggregate. Functional-currency carrying amounts and physical units per line currency are checked separately, including on currency-neutral accounts; changing exchange rates cannot hide a shortage of foreign notes. Linked reversal/replacement corrections pass or roll back as one result; standalone reversals remain guarded. Banks default to a zero permitted floor. Migration 052 permits an explicitly configured positive agreed overdraft limit in the designated account currency, or book currency for undesignated accounts; other currency buckets receive no borrowing allowance. Bank controls compare actual units to the agreed floor rather than translated carrying values. Reducing or disabling a facility retains history and rejects worsened excess borrowing. Displayed bank book balances do not assert cleared or available funds. [Cash-control tests](../../tests/cash_control_test.php) exercise these policies, access scope, idempotency and concurrent overspending. Remaining acceptance: independent accounting review, explicit classification of existing real-company money accounts, and reconciliation of any pre-existing deficits; passing tests do not supply those decisions.

For each supported policy, extend this register with the exact source/paragraph and edition, jurisdiction/entity scope, effective accounting/tax periods, interpretation, reviewer identity/date/decision, implementation version and reconciled fixture/test receipt. A source check is not approval; a successful test is not evidence of source completeness. Preserve earlier decisions when a framework or tax profile changes.

Core acceptance needs a complete sample period with reviewed opening balances, journals/adjustments, reconciled statements and period completion, including denied access, concurrent retries and corrections. Tax-module acceptance additionally needs transaction-specific rules and document-to-tax-control reconciliation. Source documents and account classifications alone cannot prove that a recognition event, valuation, exemption or disclosure is correct.

This register documents existing and in-progress local work; it adds no migration, posting behavior or permissions itself. Professional accounting review, observed user acceptance and the explicitly supported entity/framework scope remain release gates. No claim that all ICAP, ICMAP or ACCA guidance is fulfilled is made.
