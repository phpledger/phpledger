Evidence lives in [Competitive landscape](COMPETITIVE-LANDSCAPE.md) and [Pakistan market](PAKISTAN-MARKET.md). Status: **Decided** means the owner stated it in review; **Open** means a decision is still needed; **Recommended** is the reviewer's position, not a commitment. Nothing here changes the roadmap until the owner approves the corresponding document edits.
