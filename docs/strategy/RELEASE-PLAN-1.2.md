# Release plan: PHP Ledger 1.2

Drafted 20 September 2026 (evening) from the code on `master` at `9a25d63`, the published releases, the open GitHub issues and the owner decisions B18–B26. **Revised 21 September 2026** against the widened scope the owner decided the same day ([decision register](DECISION-REGISTER.md) sections L, M and N, B31 to B61) and the design packages under `docs/design/1.2-2026-09/` (distribution research, forms and reports, onboarding and packages). **Status: plan in force for the owner's "1.2 start". Nothing in it is built beyond what the status table cites, and nothing is tagged, published or deployed.** Every date is an estimate; every decision below is Taken by default under B53 and carries its reversal path; only the questions in "Open" need the owner.

1.2 is the next major release on the [roadmap](../ROADMAP.md#following-stable): the seven items the owner sequenced on 19 September 2026 (B20, B21), in that order, plus the items the owner added on 21 September without removing any. This plan says where each item stands in the code today, what remains, how the work is cut into previews and one stable release, what has to be true before 1.2.0 publishes, and which questions only the owner can answer.

## The short version

- **1.1.2 is published (20 September 2026, `3df5755`) and corrects the 1.1.1 record.** 1.1.1 had shipped migration `034_inventory_locations` and the Stock locations module unannounced and without signed metadata; 1.1.2 carries the erratum, the #88 fix and the first signed update metadata since 1.1.0. Its upgrade proof found [issue #90](https://github.com/phpledger/phpledger/issues/90): the in-app updater fails at the migrate phase (duplicate `pl_database_platform()` between the copied recovery runtime and the application, present since 1.1.0); automatic recovery restored 1.1.1 completely. **1.1.3 fixes #90 and precedes 1.2**; until then every surface says: upgrade by replacing files and running `install/migrate.php`.
- **Pilot #1 starts on 1.1.x on 1 October and stays on 1.1.x through the October, November and December closes (B28).** It never runs a preview and cuts over to 1.2.0 as a dated, backed-up event after the accountant's decision.
- **Only item (a), stock locations, is code today.** Everything else in this plan is unbuilt: trading documents, numbering, stock documents, advances, plugins, the Users module, Urdu, the container image, structured codes, contra groups, collapsible reports, owner transactions, the onboarding redesign, the counter POS over real stock, the pharmacy plugin and sample.
- **Estimate: 63 to 120 engineering days** after 1.1.3, against the 53 to 88 of the 20 September plan. The low end is the repository's demonstrated pace (the Workbench installer rebuild and the stock-locations module each took about one day, 19–20 September); the high end is the per-item ranges from the analysis maps. Four signed previews, about five weeks apart, then 1.2.0 stable with the media kit, the container image on both registries, the reviewed Urdu and the demo cutover.
- **Target for 1.2.0: Friday 19 February 2027** (earliest Friday 22 January 2027, latest Friday 26 March 2027). Nothing publishes in the week of 21 December. The 15 January 2027 target of the first draft is withdrawn.

## Shape: 1.1.3 first, four previews, one stable

The [release protocol](../RELEASE-PROTOCOL.md) is walked for every publication. Previews go on the preview channel, never move `latest` or the stable feed entry, and carry no media kit (B29). Each preview installs migrations that cannot be reverted, so previews are for testers on disposable copies (B25); the pilot never runs one. A stable installation reaches a preview by choosing "Preview" on the maintenance page and loading that preview's signed metadata (`pl_release_channel_for()` derives the channel from the installed version); the tester note on each preview says so.

| Version | Target | Contents | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1.1.3** (stable patch) | before Wed 30 Sep 2026 | Issue #90 only: the duplicate `pl_database_platform()` declaration, an update test that exercises the real migrate function, signed metadata, proof 1.1.2 → 1.1.3 through `/maintenance.php`. No new accounting behaviour. | The pilot must start on a release whose in-app upgrade path works; 1.1.3 is the pilot's baseline and is required by every later in-app proof. If it misses 30 September the pilot installs 1.1.2 by the manual procedure and takes 1.1.3 in its first week. |
| 1.1.4+ (reserved) | as needed, October–December | Pilot-blocking fixes only, about one day each; every later window shifts by the same and the shift is recorded in `AGENTS_SYNC.MD`. | The pilot brief requires one exact reviewed version per period. |

If the owner cannot sign at the planned cadence, previews collapse to fewer walks without moving the stable date; this rule is recorded so the owner is not asked per preview.

**Migration numbering** follows delivery order: 035 document series (core), 036 trading documents, 037 stock documents and warehouse kinds, 038 advances (AR and AP), 039 structured codes and contra groups, 040 users and capabilities with locale columns, 041 packages, plugin receipts and options. Owner transactions need chart-package data, not a schema change, unless M6 finds otherwise. 034 is never edited. Every migration shipped in a preview must be byte-identical at the stable tag (gate G5); a mistake found by a tester needs a new migration. Each migration also registers its label in `install_check_functions.php`, an owning manifest `migrations` entry, `tools/package-files.json` entries and the browser-installer fixture step count.

## How this plan was produced

The 20 September draft came from ten read-only analyses of the code, tests, documentation, issues and CI (one per item and release surface), three candidate plans scored by three judges, and an adversarial check of twenty load-bearing claims. This 21 September revision re-reads the register sections L to N, the three design packages and the document model, keeps every cited code fact, and re-estimates each milestone with the low end at the pace the repository demonstrated on 19–20 September and the high end at the maps' ranges. The Awan prototype (`awan-prototype/Awan-prototype.html`) remains a design aid subordinate to the accounting rules (B54). The workflow journal is outside the repository; the cited paths and line numbers are the evidence.
