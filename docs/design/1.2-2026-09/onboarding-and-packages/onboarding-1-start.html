Business setup · Stage 1 of 5

What are you setting up?

This adds one business to your installation. Nothing is created until you confirm on the last stage — you can leave and come back.

New business

Begin with no opening balances or unpaid invoices. You choose how much starting structure to bring in on the next stages — a blank chart, or a sample company's skeleton.

Most common

Bring past records

Keep prior balances and open documents. This saves the business shell first, then walks through an explicit opening cutover: chart mapping, opening trial balance and unpaid-document reconciliation.

Separate opening flow

Explore a sample company

Create a separate, clearly marked company with fictional data, isolated from your real businesses. Choose its starting depth — skeleton only or full history — on the Source stage.

For exploring, not real records

New for 1.2: a new business no longer has to start from PHP Ledger's neutral chart alone. The Source stage now also offers a sample company's skeleton — its chart of accounts, reports, forms and plugins, with none of its transactions — as a starting structure for a real business (decision B50).

Takes about two minutes.
