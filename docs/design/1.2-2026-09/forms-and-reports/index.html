PHP Ledger 1.2 — design, September 2026

Trading forms & reports

Static HTML mockups of the 1.2 trading forms and reports, sized as 1280×720 desktop frames, built on the same design tokens and component patterns as the redesign-0.5 prototype and the installer "Workbench" screens. Sample data is a fictional pharmacy/FMCG distributor, Noor Distributors (Pvt) Ltd, in PKR — two warehouses, three delivery vans, sales staff and areas. Nothing here is wired to the application; every field, button and link is inert except the trial balance's native expand/collapse.

Open each file directly in a browser. Every UI/UX choice these frames embody is recorded, with the taken option, alternatives and how to reverse it, in DECISIONS.md.

  1. 1
    Invoice editor

    Sales invoice with pack/unit line entry, a per-line discount percentage, a free-goods scheme line, sales-staff and area selectors, a warehouse selector, cash received on the invoice, and a transaction-ID style document number with the app's current series style noted alongside.

    invoice-editor.html

  2. 2
    Invoice print — A4 · Counter receipt — 80mm

    Printable letterhead invoice with a per-rate tax summary and packs/discount/free-goods columns, plus cash received and balance due; and a condensed 80mm thermal receipt for an over-the-counter cash sale.

    invoice-print-a4.html · receipt-print-80mm.html

  3. 3
    Customer statement

    Statement for one customer over a date range: opening balance, invoices, receipts, credit notes, an unapplied-credit line held as an advance, and the closing balance.

    customer-statement.html

  4. 4
    Stock issue & gate pass

    A numbered internal stock issue from a warehouse to a delivery van, distinguishing van stock already loaded from stock issued now, plus its printable A4 gate pass.

    stock-issue-gate-pass.html

  5. 5
    Stock by location

    Items and value grouped by warehouse and van, each group subtotalled, with an aggregate (all-locations) column per item and a grand total; a low-stock and a negative-stock line are flagged.

    stock-by-location.html

  6. 6
    Trial balance — collapsible

    Structured account codes in the form X-XXX-XXXXX-XX (class-group-account-sub, issue #76) with a genuinely collapsible three-level hierarchy (issue #77) using native <details> — try clicking a row.

    trial-balance-collapsible.html

  7. 7
    Receipt on account

    A single-customer receipt with an oldest-first invoice allocation preview and an unallocated remainder becoming a customer advance, plus a batch-receipts grid for recording many customers' recoveries in one voucher.

    receipt-on-account.html
