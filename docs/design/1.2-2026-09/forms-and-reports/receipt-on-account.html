Receipt on account

Two related frames: a single-customer receipt that allocates against open invoices oldest-first, with any remainder held as a customer advance; and a batch-receipts grid for recording many customers' recoveries — e.g. a salesman's end-of-day cash — in one voucher.

Batch-grid columns (Previous balance / Received / Closing balance) adapted from the Awan prototype's Receipt Voucher screen. Each receipt still posts as its own immutable voucher on Post, per PHP Ledger's posting rules — see DECISIONS.md.
