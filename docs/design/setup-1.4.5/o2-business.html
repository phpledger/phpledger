?Explain Financial year end

Financial year end

The last day of your accounting year. Reports and the year-end close follow it.

Pakistan companies usually close on 30 June; many others use 31 December.

?Explain Accounting start date

Accounting start date

The first date these books cover. Nothing can be dated before it, and the first period runs from here to the year end.

For a new business use today or the day you started trading. Bringing past records: the day after your last closed period.
