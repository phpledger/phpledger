Subject: Try PHP Ledger 1.3.0 — daily accounting through a reviewed year end

Hello,

PHP Ledger 1.3.0 adds aggregate payroll accounting, reviewed recurring and loan drafts, confidential employee links and fiscal-year close/reopen workflows to the invoices, bills, stock and reports in its self-hosted accounting application.

You can try the real installer and business onboarding at https://phpledger.com/demo/. The demo is shared: if another visitor has finished setup, use demo / DemoLedger123!. Database fields show labelled dummy values; the host connection is protected. Use fictional records only. The hourly reset removes all demo data and PHP sessions.

For a first exercise, set up a fictional business or choose a validated practice package, record a receipt and expense, then follow the resulting journal into the account statement and trial balance. The kit's guided demo provides a longer tour.

Aggregate payroll does not provide per-person pay runs, payslips or statutory filing. Loan conventions must be reviewed against the actual contract. Arabic remains a draft translation, and independent accounting/security review and real-business acceptance remain separate gates.
