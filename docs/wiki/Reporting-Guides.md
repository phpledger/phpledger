# Reporting walkthroughs

Choose a service agency, retail shop, seasonal business or distributor at [the demo](https://phpledger.com/demo/). Each private sample has 74 meaningful source records, closed 2024–2025 history, open 2026 practice and three editable drafts. Reports reconcile against 36 pinned monthly checkpoints per pack. The default capacity permits 26 further source records.

- [Daily cash and bank checks](https://phpledger.com/guides/daily-cash-check/): inspect movements and follow sources.
- [Monthly closing](https://phpledger.com/guides/monthly-closing/): review adjustments, corrections and reports.
- [Quarterly and yearly review](https://phpledger.com/guides/quarterly-yearly-review/): compare nonoverlapping periods and inspect the closing position.

The private **Sample guide** links directly into your own company's dated reports and sources. Salary, inventory and unpaid-document schedules in these sample packs are manual fictional support records, not a demonstration of the product's AR/AP or Inventory modules, and PHP Ledger does not include a payroll module.

All sample sessions and machine grants expire at the next hourly reset. Start a new sample and reconnect your client after expiry. Enter only synthetic information.
