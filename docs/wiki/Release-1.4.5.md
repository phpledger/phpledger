# PHP Ledger 1.4.5: setup you can finish in one sitting

Prepared from the versioned release notes. Publication status, exact artifacts and completed verification gates are established by the [published release](https://github.com/phpledger/phpledger/releases) and its receipt under `docs/repository/`; this page describes what the release contains.

A maintenance release from the owner's walk through the live 1.4.1 installer, business setup, Packages page and first Home, built from approved mockups.

- **Installer:** one centred column, every step inside a laptop screen, a question mark beside every field, the build starting by itself, a full step with per-host instructions when the settings file cannot be written, a 6-character password minimum with show and generate, TLS under an Advanced disclosure, and sign-in details on the completion page.
- **Installation notice:** sent by every installation; the off switch is gone. Name and email registration stays opt-in under Updates and privacy.
- **Business setup:** Start, Business, Owners and money, Features and accounts, Review. One starting point instead of two questions; sample companies as cards with their story and in-place installation from the signed directory; legal forms in the words of the country of registration, with the country recorded on the profile (migration 061); one named account per bank account, till and petty-cash float under a group that never takes an entry; opening money as capital introduced or an owner loan; the strict cash policy for new businesses; drafts that survive a visit to Packages or Modules.
- **Company profile:** the same country-aware picker and the registration fields.
- **First Home:** a getting-started guide read from the data that disappears when complete; Expense and Bill wait until an account holds money; balances per account.
- **Shell:** inlined Tabler icons in the current colour, one glyph per item, Help in the topbar, version in the user menu, no pinned sidebar footer.
- **Packages:** Installed, Sample companies, Directory and Upload tabs with compact cards and a per-business module switch.

Migration 061 follows the published chain through 060 and rewrites no data. No media kit, per the patch-release policy. See [[Getting started|Getting-Started]] and the [[Roadmap]].
