Demo use and preview terms

Explore with fictional records. The public demo is a temporary evaluation environment, refreshed hourly.

In short

  • PHP Ledger is a development preview for evaluation.
  • Use only fictional information in the public demo; its companies and access expire at the hourly reset.
  • Licence rights, optional support terms and your responsibility for self-hosted operation are separate.

Use the sample books for evaluation

Visitors share one disposable installation and the same fictional records. Enter only made-up information. Do not upload or enter personal records, real customer data, credentials or business books. Respect access controls and capacity limits and do not interfere with other visitors or the service.

Expect temporary access

Records and all PHP sessions are removed at the next scheduled hourly reset. Work may be lost and maintenance may briefly interrupt the demo. It is not a backup or a recordkeeping service. After a reset, run the actual installer again; if setup is complete, use the displayed fixed public login.

Understand the results

Reports and cash receipts are demonstrations. The cash POS records illustrative tender; it does not collect money or process card payments. The preview does not provide accounting, tax or legal advice, jurisdiction certification, statutory payroll or advanced warehouse workflows. Core AR/AP and optional Inventory with optional stock locations are bounded evaluation features; manual tax settings do not determine local obligations. Review the current scope before evaluating it.
