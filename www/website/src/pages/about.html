Accounting you can inspect and host yourself

PHP Ledger is an open-source accounting project for small businesses, maintained by Rana Mansoor Akbar Khan with support from the BixiSoft community.

In short

  • PHP Ledger is a country-neutral, self-hosted PHP accounting project maintained by Rana Mansoor Akbar Khan.
  • New project-owned code uses AGPL-3.0-or-later; a commercial licence is available.
  • 1.0.0, published 18 September 2026, was the first stable release; 1.1.2, published 20 September 2026, is the current one. Published tests describe technical evidence, not independent accounting or security review, which continue as post-release commitments.

A useful accounting core comes first

The current {{site.release.version}} package is a stable release and includes invoices, bills, partial payments, credits, ageing and reconciliation in the accounting core. Optional Purchasing and Inventory reuse its shared posting and outstanding-balance services. Manually configured tax supports inclusive or exclusive prices. Reports connect figures to their account movements and source records. 1.0.0 added guided browser installation and publisher-signed automatic updates with automatic matched backup and recovery. 1.1.0 installs like WordPress from any web folder, adds MariaDB 10.4+ support, a chosen username and an optional logo, and is the first release with signed update metadata.

The application uses PHP 8.2+, MySQL 8.4 or MariaDB 10.4+, and MeekroDB in BixiSoft's modular PHP structure. The project-owned core and current modules use AGPL-3.0-or-later; a commercial licence is available. Published 0.1.0 through 0.1.5 previews retain their MIT grants. Dependencies, fonts, photographs and historical source keep their own terms.

A workspace built around daily accounting

The interface brings a focused Home, workspace sidebar, company switcher, compact registers and reviewed document editors to the existing PHP application. Desktop and tablet are the current design targets. Mobile refinement, dark mode and full visual/accessibility acceptance remain unfinished. Read what changed in 1.1.0 and what 1.0.0 left open.

Country-neutral accounting, regional connectors

PHP Ledger is for small businesses, owners, bookkeepers and accountants across countries. Pakistan is one intended regional direction, not the main or ultimate product scope. Pakistan FBR is one planned connector alongside other tax and e-invoicing integrations. Each connector will have its own supported jurisdiction, entity and review requirements.

Free self-hosting and clear terms

The accounting core remains open source. Self-hosting for your own business is free, with no licence keys or licensing-server calls. A separate commercial licence is available on request. Future commercial modules will be named before release; native desktop and Android clients remain planned paid add-ons.

Built in the open

Contributors help improve accessibility, accounting checks, installation guidance and everyday workflows. Browse the source repository, Wiki and roadmap to see implemented work and remaining gates.

What "first stable release" means here

Use sample records while evaluating the software. Qualified accounting review, jurisdiction-specific reporting, independent security review and observed business usability remain post-release commitments, not completed acceptance. A balanced report alone does not establish compliance.

Who maintains the project, and how can you assess it?

Rana Mansoor Akbar Khan maintains PHP Ledger; the repository makes implementation, licence terms and development decisions inspectable.

The application follows BixiSoft’s modular PHP architecture with MeekroDB. That architectural background is distinct from a professional accounting qualification, audit opinion or product certification; none is claimed here. Read the contribution guidance and security policy before sending a change or a sensitive report.

Hosting and optional assistance fund their own operating costs and have separate terms. Open source gives you rights to inspect and use the code under its licence; it does not remove your responsibility to operate backups, keep the host patched or decide whether the supported workflows fit your business.

When should you wait for a later release?

Wait if you need advanced warehouse operations, payroll, automatic country tax rules, tax filing or independently accepted statements today. The current starter is intended for evaluation with sample data of its bounded accounting flows.

The learning pages publish general bookkeeping education; the research pages carry dated sources and jurisdiction-specific limits. Neither collection turns a planned feature into released software. Begin with the sample demo, record a narrow evaluation task and compare its evidence with the release notes.
