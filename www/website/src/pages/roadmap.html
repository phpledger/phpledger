The work, its evidence and what comes next

Use the published PHP Ledger release and its verification receipt to distinguish available workflows from future targets. A passing technical check is separate from independent review and acceptance on your own books.

Current direction

Keep daily entries, operational documents, period close and financial reporting connected through one exact-money ledger, with explicit review before posting.

The 1.3 accounting and platform work

People and obligations

Confidential employee records, explicit staff and driver links, aggregate payroll accruals, advances and partial settlements. Recurring, accrual and loan schedules prepare drafts with disclosed conventions.

A reviewed close

Cash counts, period-close checklists, dated reversals and a fiscal-year policy with explicit destination accounts and reviewed partner ratios. Close, roll-forward and reopen preserve traceability.

Installation and practice

Table prefixes, database TLS, MySQL 8.0.19+, draft Arabic RTL, optional technical installation notices and separately validated sample packages. The shared installer demo and both official image registries are checked during publication.

Read the workflow boundaries and check the published version. A plan, local branch or draft release is not a deployed result.

Separate acceptance gates

  • Independent accounting and security review, supervised business pilots and a real month-end close.
  • Native Arabic language and accounting review; the translation remains a draft.
  • Restricted shared-host recovery and managed-provider acceptance on the actual configuration.
  • Statutory payroll, regional tax filing, bank feeds, controlled financial write integrations and advanced industry workflows.

Aggregate payroll is not a payslip or statutory calculation engine. A loan schedule must be checked against the lender contract. Sample businesses do not establish tax or industry certification.

Optional packages

The neutral starter stays in the core. Multi-year sample histories are separate CC0 data-only packages, explicitly installed through Packages with manifest, digest and dependency checks. No sample installs an executable dependency silently. Existing companies keep their history when a source package is removed.

Future specialist plugins have separate delivery and review gates. The current plugin runtime and private secret store are a foundation, not evidence that a provider connector or industry system has shipped.

History and participation

Earlier release records remain available in project news and the release archive. The repository roadmap records detailed decisions and sequence.
