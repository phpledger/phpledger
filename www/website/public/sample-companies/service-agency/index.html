2026 · Your practice year

January contains seeded support and a bank receipt. February's receipt and expenses remain editable drafts.

Business decision
Choose or explicitly create the payer or payee, check the payment date and posted balance, and review before posting.
Accounting treatment
A draft changes no balance. Fund petty cash with a real transfer before paying from it; do not record invented income to cover a shortfall. Use invoice settlement when paying an existing open item.
What to inspect
Compare petty cash before and after posting; trace the linked party and journal. Practice changes belong only to your isolated company.
