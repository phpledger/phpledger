Open-source accounting software: compare five different product families

Key takeaways

Open-source licensing answers a permissions question; selecting accounting software requires a separate workflow and evidence review.

  • Read the current licence for the exact release, including optional apps and bundled components.
  • Decide whether you need a general ledger, invoices, stock or a connected operational suite.
  • Evaluate the actual hosted or self-hosted edition, not an unspecified product name.
  • Test recovery, corrections and reconciliations with known examples.
  • PHP Ledger is a development preview with core invoices/bills/payments, optional one-location Inventory and Purchasing, and manual tax configuration. Advanced operations and regional filing remain outside its scope.

What does open source mean here?

We use the Open Source Initiative's definition, rather than treating downloadable source as sufficient.

The Open Source Definition and approved-licence list, checked on 16 September 2026, provide the classification used in this guide. A project may publish source under terms that restrict production use or a competing service. That can still be a valid commercial offering, but it is a different permission model. Separate this distinction from whether the application is useful, well supported or affordable.

Licences also differ within open source. Copyleft obligations, network interaction and redistribution deserve attention when a developer modifies software or an accountant hosts it for clients. Review the release's actual notices and obtain advice where the intended arrangement is unclear. A permissive or copyleft label is not a certificate of accounting accuracy, security or compliance. Conversely, charging for hosting or support does not itself make an open-source project non-open-source.

Which labels are easily confused?

Free price, source visibility, self-hosting and open-source permission describe different properties.

Open source
Software distributed under terms meeting the Open Source Definition, with the relevant obligations still applying.
Source available
Source can be inspected, but its licence may restrict uses that an open-source licence permits.
Self-hosted
The application runs on infrastructure controlled by the operator; this does not specify the licence or external dependencies.
Hosted service
A provider operates the installation under a service agreement, which may involve open-source or proprietary software.

For example, a team can pay someone to host an open-source application, or self-host a commercially licensed one. A free invoice app can still lack a double-entry ledger. Write these properties into separate columns instead of forcing them into a single free-versus-paid score.

Family one: business accounting applications

Start here when the central problem is maintaining business books and their supporting transaction records.

Bigcapital documents accounting, inventory and Docker self-hosting under AGPL licensing. FrontAccounting is a GPL-licensed PHP accounting project with sales and purchasing scope. LedgerSMB describes web-based double-entry accounting and ERP on a different application stack. These primary project sources were checked on 16 September 2026. They establish a shortlist, not an installed acceptance result.

Akaunting also belongs in a practical accounting shortlist, but its current BSL licence means it should not be silently classified as equivalent to OSI-approved licences. Its Double-Entry app is a paid offering. Both sources were checked on 16 September 2026. Include the exact edition and required apps in the evaluation. The Akaunting comparison explains the distinction without assuming that a paid app is a defect.

What should this family demonstrate?

Require a traceable connection between the business event, journal, detailed balances and financial reports.

Test a sale, a purchase, a payment and an adjustment appropriate to the supported scope. Ask how the system prevents duplicate posting and how it handles a closed period. If customer or supplier balances are required, reconcile their detailed schedules to the general ledger. If stock is required, include quantity and valuation checks. A balanced report alone cannot establish that every business event was captured or assigned to the right period.

Family two: ERP suites with accounting

Choose this family when accounting needs to remain connected to purchasing, sales, stock or other operational processes.

ERPNext, Dolibarr and Odoo publish primary repositories describing broader business suites; these were checked on 16 September 2026. Their editions, modules and deployment models differ. Do not assume that a feature shown in a hosted demonstration is included in every community package. Name the release and module combination before comparing capabilities or quoting licence terms.

An ERP may be appropriate for a small distributor with complex stock and credit transactions. It may be unnecessary for a simpler operation with well-defined external systems. Evaluate the connection between records: an order that becomes a delivery and invoice, a purchase that becomes stock and a liability, or a return that affects both stock and money. The benefit is reduced handoff work when the implementation fits. The cost is more configuration, training and operational responsibility.

Read the ERPNext comparison and Dolibarr comparison for specific evaluation questions. This guide does not infer that ERP is inherently slow or expensive. It also does not promise a fixed server size: dataset, workload, release and deployment method all affect capacity.

Family three: invoicing and billing tools

Choose an invoicing tool when the task is issuing and following up customer documents, while identifying who maintains the books.

The Invoice Ninja repository, checked on 16 September 2026, describes invoice, quote, project and time-tracking software and currently identifies its source-available character. That is useful product scope, but the name accounting software should not be applied without checking the required ledger functions. Other invoice-focused projects also need their own current licence and feature review; this guide does not infer their terms from their category.

A freelancer might need attractive invoices and payment tracking while an accountant maintains the ledger elsewhere. That can be a deliberate arrangement. Confirm the export, cutoff and reconciliation procedure. An invoice list does not by itself demonstrate a balance sheet, controlled periods or a complete audit trail. If the tool is only one part of the records process, the handoff is part of the implementation and needs an owner.

Family four: personal and plain-text ledgers

Personal-finance tools can be technically capable while still being a poor fit for shared business operations.

Firefly III, checked on 16 September 2026, describes itself as a personal-finance manager. Personal and plain-text accounting tools can suit users who want direct control over records and a particular working style. Their presence in an accounting search result should not be read as evidence of business invoicing, staff permissions or jurisdiction support.

A technically skilled sole operator may prefer files and command-line reports. A shop with multiple clerks and a separate reviewer may require forms, role boundaries and document approval. Neither preference is inherently superior. Test who can make changes, how mistakes are discovered and how another person reconstructs the books. If editing a file requires skills the regular operator lacks, the workflow needs support or a different interface.

Family five: proprietary and managed alternatives

Keep non-open-source options on a practical shortlist when they meet requirements the open-source candidates cannot yet satisfy.

This family is included as a decision category, not as a claim about a particular vendor's current plan. A business may reasonably prefer a service that transfers infrastructure administration to a provider. Compare the agreement, exports, data location, account access and exit procedure. If local electronic invoicing is mandatory, request evidence for the actual entity and workflow rather than assuming that a country name in a feature list is sufficient.

The ongoing cost includes staff effort and implementation, not only licence or subscription charges. Self-hosting may reduce one fee while adding operational work. A managed product may simplify administration while limiting customization. Record these tradeoffs explicitly. This guide does not publish price rankings because the relevant plan, region and required modules need a dated individual quotation.

How can you keep the trial reproducible?

Record the release, settings, data and expected result before comparing two systems.

  1. Write the essential workflows and assign a reviewer for each.
  2. Create one shared fictional example with known totals and dates.
  3. Install the exact candidate editions in isolated environments.
  4. Record normal, rejected, corrected and restored outcomes.
  5. Document manual steps, missing functions and unresolved questions.
  6. Approve a migration only after reconciling an agreed opening position.

Where PHP Ledger loses

The current preview lacks complete business subledgers and broader operational functions available in established alternatives.

PHP Ledger is a bounded accounting starter; it does not claim complete ERP, advanced warehouse or country tax coverage. Its cash POS showcase does not provide stock valuation or cost of goods sold. Its currency foundations do not provide period-end revaluation or group consolidation. Its party schema does not provide customer or vendor operational screens. These distinctions should remain visible when evaluating the current product, even when backend mechanics have passed tests.

Its narrower PHP core may fit a technical evaluation of bookkeeping and source history. It does not justify asking a business to depend on unsupported workflows. The current project-owned code is AGPL-3.0-or-later; self-hosting uses no licence keys or licensing-server calls. Check the download notes and runtime requirements. The tested databases are MySQL 8.4 and MariaDB 10.4 or newer, so compatibility cannot be inferred from a generic shared-hosting advertisement.

Questions and recommendation

Choose the smallest supported scope that covers the real work, with a credible path for review and recovery.

Does open source remove the need for paid support?

No. It changes software permissions. Administration, training, implementation and accounting review still require time and expertise, whether provided internally or purchased.

Should every business self-host?

No. Self-hosting makes sense when its control benefits and operational responsibilities fit the organization. An unattended server is not a data-governance strategy.

Use the FrontAccounting and Bigcapital comparisons to refine a business-accounting shortlist. Read the research method and evidence gaps before treating any category as a quality ranking.
